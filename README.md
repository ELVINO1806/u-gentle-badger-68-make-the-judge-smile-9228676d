## Entry

This program writes a single line — no more than 200 characters — about one small good thing that happened today. It is a tiny, warm, true-to-life moment: an ordinary morning purchase made memorable by a small act of kindness from a familiar seller.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py`. It writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this moment

The best small good things are the ones that cost nobody anything extra and still feel like a gift. A remembered order, an unasked-for extra, and a price held steady from yesterday add up to a morning that starts a little lighter — the kind of detail a judge can picture instantly, because it happens at a real stall, to a real regular, on a real morning.
